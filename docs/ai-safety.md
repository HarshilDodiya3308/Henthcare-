# AI Safety Design

## Safety Boundaries

The AI assistant must not claim diagnostic certainty, prescribe prescription medicines independently, replace doctors, or clear emergencies.

## Triage Classes

- **Low Concern:** general supportive information and monitoring.
- **Medical Review:** recommend professional evaluation.
- **Urgent:** recommend immediate professional or emergency care.

## Offline AI Layer

Offline support may include language detection, intent classification, structured symptom flows, preloaded validated health information, safety rules, and lightweight local models.

## Required User Messaging

Responses should be short, understandable, and include professional-care escalation when symptoms are serious, worsening, or unclear. Traditional remedies must never be presented as guaranteed cures.
